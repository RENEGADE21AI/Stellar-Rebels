# Stellar Rebels Arcade pacing

Each stage is saved and replayable on its own. **Next Flight** moves directly from a settled victory into the next location. Score, time, targets, Salvage and unlocks remain visible on a concise results screen. Replay and Hangar are secondary actions. The combat HUD names the current section instead of counting up to the boss.

## Six-stage audit

| Stage | Read of the previous route | Production choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vesper Orbit | Its 147 seconds of regularly spaced formations introduce important roles, with most of the change in scale concentrated in the Warden. | Make this the representative pacing revision. Fight from the opening second, introduce a heavy target after the scouts, build combined threats, fly past a cruiser and arrive at the Warden at 126 seconds. |
| Asteroid Belt | Separate debris rows punctuate the sweep-and-dive waves and prepare pilots for Harrow. | Keep the debris crossings as the stage's own change of rhythm. The tighter Harrow silhouette leaves the seam and seed fire legible. |
| Relay Station | Shield connections and command wings create useful priority decisions. Several stretches return to the same group at a regular interval. | Keep the escort and relay identity. Group the presentation around the escort screen, command wings and final approach. Further sequence changes need a direct play comparison. |
| Red Sun | Aimed lanes already interrupt the rhythm of repeated Gunner ladders and create a positional problem before the Rail Ship. | Keep that pressure and the visible gap between locks. The Rail Ship stays compact and its emitter remains physically aligned. |
| Outer Orbit | Phantoms preview two kinds of fire and gravity makes lanes shift before the Wraith. | Retain the warning and release. The two-realm rules stay inside the Wraith fight and its remodelled hull keeps the same size across both realms. |
| The Flagship | Dense combinations, debris and rails form a climb that suits the finale. An oversized final hull reduced visible space. | Preserve the escalation and tune hull and attachment scale. Keep the final payoff at the fleet encounter. |

## Vesper Orbit beats

- **Approach, 0–16 seconds.** Scout chevrons and a sweep across the lanes establish aiming and Ghost Energy. The first Weapon Power drops at 14 seconds.
- **Blockade screen, 16–42 seconds.** Gunners, a bending wing and a linked group introduce stronger targets with space to react. Barrier follows before pressure rises.
- **Fighter wings, 42–82 seconds.** Paired heavy threats, dives and sweeps mix lanes. A repair drop and broad scout pass lead into the crossing.
- **Cruiser crossing, 82–103 seconds.** The cruiser crosses the upper field on the same camera. Its marked guns charge and fire independently. Each can be destroyed as the ship passes. A thin fighter screen gives a reason to change lanes, and the cruiser leaves even if the pilot prioritizes survival.
- **Final approach, 103–126 seconds.** Linked fire and a last crossing build again. Overdrive reaches the field as the Warden arrives.
- **Warden and resolution.** The Warden is a distinct closing fight that builds on the gun targeting learned during the cruiser. Victory settles before Next Flight opens the next stage.

Comparison uses browser play across both versions, a real-time stock Fighter flight, and focused compact and tall evidence at the crossing and boss. Retention, perceived excitement and difficulty for first-time players remain questions for human playtests.
