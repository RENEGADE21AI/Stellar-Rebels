# Stellar Rebels legacy strengths and regression audit

The reference builds are Star Strike RUSH at `214cd64ec37c50d8921b0d0d0e188ad8038956b4` and Stellar Rebels before this restoration at `b34b16ab9ffa383c0fd7ee6d9614a3ea35588324`. Both commit IDs come from the live sites' version manifests. I played both opening flights in Chromium at phone scale. The restored candidate is compared using the same browser sizes and ordinary entry points. [Star Strike RUSH](https://star-strike-rush.web.app/) · [Stellar Rebels](https://stellar-rebels.web.app/)

| Element | What I observed | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting into combat | Star Strike RUSH's title Play goes straight into the flight, following its separate first-run tutorial choice. Stellar Rebels routed the same action through Stage Select every time. | Play starts the next unlocked and uncleared stage with the equipped ship. After the whole route clears, Play replays the finale. Stage Select remains available for replays and Endless. |
| Steering and overlays | The original joystick and charge control took up space around the ship. Stellar's relative drag was responsive, but the full-width panel covered the top of the field, especially at 320×568. | Keep relative drag and keyboard support. Put the hull, score, stage, Shift, pause and boss health in one short rail above the canvas. The combat field keeps the same scale from opening wave to boss. |
| Fighters and pacing | Legacy fighters descend, weave and escape while pressure moves through the field. In the old Stellar build most ships visibly settled at a fixed altitude, so entering and retreating waves could feel like rows of turrets. | Restore forward movement with distinct speeds and passes. The Gunner slows briefly, the Sweeper crosses lanes, the Diver signals its dive, and the Command Ship can lead a synchronized wing. Group timings in the opening three stages now vary more. |
| Surviving fighters | The original retains the same craft on the return, then picks an unrestricted lane. The previous Stellar code already supported a different lane, but the slower default motion made the pass easy to miss. | A selected Scout or Diver returns once. Hull and group reward credit carry through the pass. A seeded, separated lane and a changed approach make the second pass visible. Returners remain harmless on the edge and peel away before set pieces. |
| Fighter, enemies and supplies | The original Fighter's bright canopy and the red Scout's simple silhouette read particularly well on a dark field. The two basic original delta enemies have a similar profile. The newer Gunner, Diver, and Sweeper have clearer role silhouettes as a group. | Restore the original Fighter and red Scout, keep the other distinct forms, and retain the adapted original pickup art. The opening wing can drop Power early. The timed supply is available to a new player who misses the wing. |
| Contact and feedback | Many previous hitboxes were single circles centered on padded art. A projectile could miss a solid wing or be stopped by the transparent area around a boss. Legacy's heavier explosions used layered transients. | Use opaque sprite geometry for enemy craft and rocks with a deliberately forgiving pilot cockpit. Swept projectiles take movement and visible heading into account. Impacts spark near the contact point and destroyed craft have a short second sound layer. |
| Cruiser, bosses and Wraith | Simply enlarging the ship takes away dodge room. The crossing Cruiser and the individual Warden, Carrier, and Flagship modules provide better targets when their origins can be read. Wraith's previous dark finish concealed much of its wing structure. | Keep the arena and distinct mechanics. Move removable hardware so it is visible and shootable from below. Retain the original Wraith encounter identity and repaint its armor with bright metal edges. Physical Ghost Shift phases through physical fire. Wraith Hop changes the interacting realm, and the lit core takes damage in that realm. The Gunship's inner pair stays close enough to hit that core when lined up. |
| Long-term play | The former Star Strike RUSH release paired its arcade flight with a larger progression and account layer. Stellar's stage route, mastery, earned sidegrades, and replay format are easier to understand. | Preserve progression, personal records, and low-cost deterministic input capture. Run seeds vary per attempt. This restoration shipped as `arcade-6`. The current `arcade-8` release includes the equipped Special, and the CLI verifies `arcade-5` through `arcade-8` recordings against their corresponding simulations. |

## Lessons from arcade shooters

- **1943: The Battle of Midway.** The supplied aircraft image shows damage local to the engines. In the ship clip, the pilot still has a usable route beside an enormous target. I used that distinction for the Cruiser and removable hardware, and took the idea of earning a temporary weapon in combat from the arcade power-up rules. Existing Hull and Ghost Energy keep their separate roles. [Capcom's arcade kit manual](https://www.mamechannel.it/files_free/arcade_manuals_unpacked/1943.pdf)
- **Zanac and CAVE.** Zanac designer Masamitsu Hirono emphasized intentional openings in dense patterns. CAVE's Tsuneki Ikeda described how the appearance and shape of a volley contribute to the player's enjoyment. The application here is to vary lane pressure and firing directions, preserve an exit from a telegraph, and let bullet shape and color carry information at phone size. [Zanac developer interview](https://shmuplations.com/zanac/) · [CAVE developer interview](https://shmuplations.com/cave15th/)
- **Gunvein and Blue Revolver.** NGDEV describes converting aggressive play into temporary combat resources. Stellar Circle gives players score incentives for picking apart large targets and offers legibility options. Stellar already has Ghost Energy from attacks and local mastery for disabling both Warden turrets. The openings and boss modules make those existing incentives easier to see in action. [NGDEV's Gunvein release page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2025840/Gunvein/) · [Stellar Circle's Blue Revolver page](https://stellarcircle.itch.io/blue-revolver)
- **Stage structure.** Developer Attilio Carotenuto describes introducing a stage's idea in an approachable wave, adding variations, and using the boss as its culmination. Vesper Orbit teaches tracking and returning threats before the Cruiser, Asteroid Belt alternates sweeps and clear seams, and Relay Station establishes support ships before the carrier's shield nodes. [Attilio Carotenuto on shmup level design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-smart-meaningful-shmups)

## Artwork and collision verification

Wraith's repaint used the built-in image generator with the previous original Wraith as the edit target. The brief kept the overhead batwing silhouette, crystal and muzzle aligned, added silver edges and reflected violet light, and asked for a transparent, isolated asset that reads at a small size. The editable reference is [wraith-refined.png](../source-art/stellar/2d/wraith-refined.png). The physical and ghost exports share their alpha mask.

To view the live collision geometry locally, build the web artifact, run `node scripts/serve-static.js dist-stellar 4198 --hitboxes`, and open [the local collision view](http://127.0.0.1:4198/?hitboxes=1). White marks the pilot's damageable cockpit, green the pickup region, orange enemy hull or armor, yellow boss hardware and the Wraith core, and red active hostile geometry. The overlay is served by the loopback development server. Changes to shipping sprite bytes trigger a collision-geometry build check.

## Web release and review

The published restoration is `aa2b02f489bdf025e5b7de78774dae2cf4f047dc` with pack `d639edd44cd48bd`. Its 9.05 MiB flight pack is within the Hosting limit. A browser flight paused on pack `773308a3be46371` retained its prior sprite offline, resumed, and offered the new pack at the menu. The saved reduced-motion preference survived activation. A rollback copy of the prior live pack remains in `work/portable-b34b16ab9ffa/game`. [Live version](https://stellar-rebels.web.app/version.json)

The new rail occupies 50 CSS pixels above the canvas at the two tested phone sizes. The 320×568 view uses 0.67448 CSS pixels per world unit, about 9% below the former full-canvas scale. The 390×844 view retains 1.015625. The scale stays fixed through the Cruiser and bosses. On both sizes a visible lance through the Fighter's outer wing preserved five hull and a cockpit hit dropped it to four.

The primary checks included 27 final browser and simulation tests, eight tests against the published website, separate real-time touch clears of Vesper Orbit and Outer Orbit, and a five-minute Endless run. A controlled fixed-tick browser campaign cleared all six stages with normal settlement and unlocks. An earlier check exposed an alignment problem with the Gunship's inner guns against Wraith. The narrower pair passed the corrected fight. Separate Chromium and WebKit replays verified under the new rules, and archived recordings verified under the prior rules. These captures and timings come from desktop browsers configured at phone dimensions.

One independent comprehensive review examined the three builds at both sizes. Its discovery score was 8.4/10 on `aae864d`. It found the Wraith's incoming realm could clip, including when the adjacent pilot Hop pointed the other way. Its targeted recheck of `aa2b02f` observed both naturally scheduled transitions at both sizes with seven hull and a five-digit score. Both destinations fit and arena placement stayed fixed. Web review is complete. Physical-device behavior, native runtime and a matched listening judgment remain unverified. No mobile build or additional paid development action occurred.
