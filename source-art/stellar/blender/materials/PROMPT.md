# Stellar Rebels material source

The built-in ImageGen tool produced one original atlas for Blender material mapping. The selected quadrants are packed into the editable workbench and applied to modeled armor only.

Create an original texture atlas for Blender PBR base-color on layered spacecraft plates in a professional sci-fi arcade game, Stellar Rebels. Square flat 2048-style composition, exactly four equal edge-to-edge square quadrants in a precise 2×2 grid, no border or gaps. Orthographic perpendicular top-down studio scan of real machined SPACECRAFT MATERIALS. Entirely flat artwork suitable for UV mapping, no directional light, no perspective, no text, no numbers, no logos, no ships, no characters, no borders, no outer backgrounds. Each quadrant is independent. Upper left: weathered bone-ivory aerospace ceramic tiled plating with staggered angular fine dark separation lines, a few nearly black small chips, very restrained burnished copper traces, gray join strips, uneven tiny engraved fasteners. Upper right: dark cool graphite and charcoal titanium armored plates with fine geometric dark recesses, subtle cool brushed highlights, tiny silver bolts and crisp chamfer seams, strong documentary realism, no emissive lines. Lower left: blackened burnt copper and brick-red hard-anodized heavy alloy with narrowly spaced curved horizontal scuff marks, engineered hatches and tiny dark screws, attractive medium-dark color with parts of each patch approaching graphite, restrained orange showing through weathering. Lower right: midnight navy and cool cyan dark glass composite with subtle thin dark lamination seams and scuffed charcoal edges, no glare and no emission. Detail fills every quadrant, realistically restrained high-frequency micro-pattern with believable panel geometry. Realistic AAA game material scanning, shadows minimized, neutral exposure. This is a reusable material map, not a layout design or a sprite.
